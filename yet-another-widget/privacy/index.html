Summary

Yet Another Widget is an independent Android home-screen widget published by Ramy Baheeg. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, an analytics SDK, Firebase Crashlytics, or an app-operated account or telemetry service. Most information stays on your device.

Information may be sent to the weather provider you select, or to an external website you choose to open. Network providers may receive technical connection information such as an IP address.

Calendar

If you grant calendar access and enable calendar features, the app reads upcoming events using Android’s calendar APIs. Event titles, times, locations, and related information may be cached in the app’s private storage to show the widget. The app does not send calendar contents to an app-operated server.

Location and weather

If you select device-based weather location and grant permission, the app requests approximate (coarse) location while an app screen is visible. The last coordinates may be stored locally and reused for later weather refreshes without repeatedly requesting location while the app is closed.

The app supports the U.S. National Weather Service for U.S. locations and WeatherAPI.com for other supported locations. Selecting WeatherAPI.com requires your own API key, stored locally and sent to WeatherAPI.com for requests. No shared WeatherAPI.com key is bundled. Weather requests share the relevant location with your selected provider, subject to that provider’s privacy terms.

If you search for a custom place, Android’s Geocoder may be used. Its implementation and any network processing depend on your Android device and service provider.

Notifications and current media

With your explicit Android notification-listener authorization, optional widget features may read notification metadata and information about the currently playing media, such as titles, source apps, artists, and albums. The app uses local metadata for display and does not send it to an app-operated server.

If notification-listener access is revoked, the app is designed to stop showing cached notification content that requires that authorization.

Wallpaper preview

On Android 12L and earlier, an optional in-app widget preview may request legacy storage access to display your existing wallpaper behind the preview. This is not used to upload wallpaper data. The legacy permission is not requested on Android 13 or later.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Preferences, widget layout, cached calendar information, weather coordinates, a WeatherAPI.com key if supplied, and optional notification/media display metadata are stored within the app’s private local storage. Android app-data backup is disabled.

Locally stored data remains until it is replaced or cleared by app behavior, you clear app storage through Android settings, or you uninstall the app. The app does not provide an account because no account is required.

External services and links

Enabled weather requests contact the chosen weather provider. Opening a project feedback link or documentation page takes you to an external site. Those external services may process connection information under their own privacy policies.

Relevant services include the U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov), WeatherAPI.com, your Android device’s Geocoder implementation, and GitHub if you open the project’s feedback links.

Data sale and advertising

The app does not sell personal data or use personal or sensitive data for advertising. It does not include an advertising SDK.

Changes and questions

This notice will be updated if the app’s data practices or supported third-party services materially change. For general privacy-policy questions, use the public privacy-policy issue tracker at github.com/masterramy/masterramy.github.io/issues. Do not post private calendar events, locations, API keys, or other personal information in a public issue.
